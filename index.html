FAQ

What does a Global Talent Visa offer?

Immigram offers a platform that helps Global Talent visa applicants in digital tech prepare and submit their applications, which includes assisting with:

  • No job offer required – You can apply without having a confirmed job in the UK;

  • Freedom to work – You can work for any employer, change jobs, or be self-employed;

  • No salary or sponsorship restrictions – You are not tied to a specific salary threshold or an employer's sponsorship;

  • Fast track to settlement (Indefinite Leave to Remain) – You can apply for permanent residency after 3 years (if applying as a recognised leader) or 5 years (if applying as a promising talent);

  • Family members can join you – Your partner and children can come to the UK as your dependants.

Who can apply for the Global Talent Visa?

The Global Talent Visa is open to individuals who are recognized as leaders or potential leaders in one of the approved fields: academia or research, arts and culture, and digital technology. Applicants must be aged 18 or over and receive an endorsement from an approved body (such as Tech Nation, The Royal Society, or Arts Council England) confirming their exceptional talent or promise.

What does a Global Talent Visa offer?

Global Talent candidates in digital tech will need to attach a completed Visa application form, which includes:

  • A Personal Statement;

  • A CV, containing your experience, contributions to the relevant field, publications, etc;

  • At least 3 letters of recommendation from recognised leaders in the tech industry;

  • Up to 10 pieces of evidence in relation to the relevant Eligibility Criteria.

What does Immigram offer?

Immigram offers a platform that helps Global Talent visa applicants in digital tech prepare and submit their applications, which includes assisting with:

  • A Personal Statement;

  • A CV highlighting experience and contributions;

  • At least 3 recommendation letters from tech industry leaders;

  • Up to 10 pieces of evidence meeting the Eligibility Criteria.
